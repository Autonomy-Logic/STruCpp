## Licensing and the Contributor License Agreement

STruC++ is licensed under the GPLv3, and its runtime library under the GPLv3 with the STruC++ Runtime Library Exception (see `LICENSE`, `COPYING` and `COPYING.RUNTIME`).

- **Contributor License Agreement.** By ticking the Contributor License Agreement checkbox in the pull request template, you agree that your contribution is licensed to Autonomy Logic Inc. under the terms of the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). The CLA check fails until the box is ticked. You keep the copyright on your contribution.
- **Always available under the GPLv3.** Every contribution accepted here stays available under the GPLv3, in addition to any other licence Autonomy offers.
- **SPDX header.** Every new source file starts with the SPDX identifier used by its neighbours (`GPL-3.0-only`, or `GPL-3.0-only WITH STruCpp-runtime-exception` for runtime library files) and the Autonomy copyright line.
- **Third-party code.** Do not add third-party code unless its licence is compatible with both the GPLv3 and the commercial licences of the OpenPLC runtimes. Ask in the pull request when in doubt.

## Workflow

1. Branch from `development` and open the pull request against `development`.
2. Fill in the pull request template.
